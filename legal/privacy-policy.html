4. How Your Data Is Shared

Your practice logs are private by default. A log is visible only to you unless you choose to share it.

When you link to a Teacher or Studio and share a session, we apply a layered, minimal-by-default consent model:

  • A linked Teacher sees only a minimal summary of a shared session: date, practice type, duration, mood, and only pain/injured body markers — not your other body markers.
  • Your written notes are shared only if you separately turn on note-sharing.
  • Your photos and videos are shared only if you separately mark each file as shared.
  • A linked Studio sees only aggregate statistics (such as totals and counts) and never your individual sessions. This is enforced at the database level.
  • Peer activity (following other practitioners) is visible only after a follow request is accepted.

You can disconnect from a teacher or studio at any time, which stops future sharing.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

We keep your personal data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile and associated records are deleted, and related records are removed automatically through linked deletion, except for limited backups retained for 90 days and any data we must keep to meet legal obligations.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete your data ("right to erasure");
  • port your data to another service;
  • withdraw consent you previously gave (for example, stop sharing with a teacher);
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • lodge a complaint with your data-protection regulator. In Hong Kong: Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data (PCPD). In Singapore: Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC).

To exercise any of these, contact us at yogawithagnesc@gmail.com. Many of these actions — making a log private, un-sharing media, disconnecting from a teacher, or deleting your account — can also be done directly in the app.

8. How We Protect Your Data

  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our infrastructure providers.
  • Access is controlled by row-level security, so each user's data is technically isolated — for example, studios are blocked at the database layer from reading individual practice logs.
  • We limit what is shared by default and require explicit opt-in for sensitive items.

No online service can be perfectly secure, and we do not claim end-to-end encryption or any security certification we have not obtained. We will notify you and any regulator of a data breach where the law requires.
