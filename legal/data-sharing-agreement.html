Lumen — Data Sharing Agreement

Version: 0.2
Last updated: 2026-08-22

Important — not legal advice. This document has been reviewed and is accurate to the product as built. It remains not a substitute for review by a qualified attorney licensed in your governing jurisdiction before use. See the Privacy Policy for full detail on how Lumen handles your data.

This Data Sharing Agreement governs what information a Student agrees to share with a linked Teacher or Studio (the "Recipient") when connecting via join code. It is complementary to the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

1. Scope of This Agreement

When you link to a Teacher or Studio by entering their join code, you consent to the sharing of specific practice information described in this agreement. This consent is:

  • Explicit — you must check the consent box at linking time;
  • Revocable — you can unlink at any time, stopping all future sharing;
  • Minimal-by-default — only the minimum necessary information is visible to the Recipient unless you separately opt in to further sharing (e.g., notes, media).

2. What a Teacher Can See

When you link to a Teacher, they can view a summary of each practice session you share:

  • Date of the session
  • Practice type (e.g., Vinyasa, Running, Strength)
  • Duration (minutes)
  • Mood (e.g., Energized, Calm, Sore)
  • Pain or injured body markers only — not your other muscle-feeling markers (e.g., if you marked your lower back as "sore," the teacher sees this; if you marked your chest as "mobile," the teacher does not see this)

A Teacher cannot see:

  • Your written notes (unless you separately opt in to note-sharing);
  • Your photos or videos (unless you separately mark each file as shared);
  • Non-pain body markers (e.g., tension, mobility, flexibility);
  • Your overall activity history or statistics;
  • Other students' information.

3. What a Studio Can See

A Studio never sees individual practice records. Instead, they see only aggregate statistics about their linked students as a group:

  • Total number of active students;
  • Total sessions logged across all linked students;
  • Total minutes practiced;
  • Aggregate trends (e.g., "students logged 40 hours this week").

This restriction is enforced at the database level, not merely by policy. A Studio cannot request or access individual student records even if both parties consent.

4. Notes and Media Sharing

Your written notes and media (photos, videos) are never shared by default, even with a linked Teacher or Studio. To share:

  • Notes: You must separately toggle on "Share notes with my Teacher" in your profile or in the log itself. This setting applies to all future logs with notes.
  • Media: You must separately mark each photo or video as "Shared with Teacher" or "Public." This is a per-file choice; sharing one photo does not automatically share others.

5. Limitations on Use

When you share information with a Teacher or Studio, they agree to use it only for:

  • Instruction and feedback — commenting on your practice, form, and progress;
  • Coordination — scheduling classes, discussing your needs, or facilitating group practice.

They must not use it for:

  • Marketing or advertising — using your data to promote their services or products;
  • Resale or onward disclosure — sharing or selling your information to third parties;
  • Profiling or unrelated purposes — analyzing your data for purposes beyond instruction (e.g., demographic profiling, recommendation algorithms not tied to your practice).

6. Revocation and Unlinking

You can revoke this agreement at any time by unlinking from the Teacher or Studio in your profile. Unlinking:

  • Stops all future sharing immediately;
  • Does not retroactively delete data the Teacher may have already accessed or saved (you are responsible for requesting deletion if needed);
  • Does not affect past feedback or data they provided to you.

7. Duration

This agreement remains in effect for as long as your linkage is active. It terminates automatically if:

  • You unlink from the Recipient;
  • The Recipient deactivates or closes their account;
  • You close your account (all linkages are revoked, and your data is deleted as described in the Privacy Policy).

8. Acknowledgment

By clicking "I agree" at the time of linking, you confirm that you have read and understood this Data Sharing Agreement and that you consent to the sharing of your practice data as described herein.
