Lumen — Data Sharing Agreement

Version: 0.2
Last updated: 2026-08-22

This Agreement explains what happens when you link your Lumen account to another account using a join code. You accept it at the moment you link. You can disconnect at any time.

1. What linking does

Linking creates a consent-based connection so that a Teacher or Studio can support your practice. It does not make your data public and does not share your private logs. It only enables the specific, limited sharing described below — and only for sessions you choose to share.

There are two kinds of link:

  • Student → Teacher / Studio — you agree to share limited practice data, as set out here. (Consent shown when linking: "I agree to share my practice data with this Teacher / Studio in accordance with the Data Sharing Agreement.")
  • Teacher → Studio — you confirm you teach at the Studio and agree to appear on its schedule. No student practice data is shared by this link. (Consent shown when linking: "I confirm I teach at this Studio and agree to appear on its schedule.")

The rest of this Agreement describes the Student → Teacher / Studio link.

2. What a linked Teacher CAN see

Your practice logs are private by default. Even after you link, a Teacher sees a session only if you share it — and then only a minimal summary:

Shared by default (for a session you share) Shared only with a separate opt-in
Practice date Your written notes / reflection — hidden unless you turn on note-sharing
Practice type Your photos and videos — hidden unless you mark each file as shared
Duration
Mood
Only pain / injured body markers

3. What a linked Teacher CANNOT see

  • Any log you keep private.
  • Body markers other than pain/injured (for example, tight or sore) — these are not shared.
  • Your notes, unless you separately turn on note-sharing.
  • Your photos or videos, unless you separately mark a file as shared.

4. What a linked Studio can see

A Studio account sees only aggregate statistics — such as totals and counts across its linked members — and never an individual student's sessions, notes, media, or body data. This limit is enforced at the database level, not just by policy.

5. How the recipient may use your data

A linked Teacher or Studio may use what you share only to support your practice — for instruction, feedback, and coordinating classes. They may not:

  • use it for marketing or advertising,
  • sell or rent it, or
  • disclose it to anyone else,

except as you direct or as required by law. These obligations are also part of the Terms of Service.

6. Withdrawing consent / disconnecting

You can disconnect from a Teacher or Studio at any time in the app. When you disconnect:

  • future sharing stops immediately, and
  • the Teacher/Studio no longer has access to your practice data through Lumen.

Information a recipient was permitted to view while you were linked may remain in anything they had already noted outside Lumen; Lumen cannot control copies made outside the Service. If you have concerns, contact us at yogawithagnesc@gmail.com.

7. More information

This Agreement works together with the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. If anything here is unclear, contact yogawithagnesc@gmail.com before linking.
